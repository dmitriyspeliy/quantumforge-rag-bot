# Sovereign Vael Drakos

Sovereign Vael Drakos secretly operated as Nox Sidren while publicly rising through the politics of the Solar Concord. He manipulated war, fear, and emergency powers to transform the Solar Concord into the Orion Dominion.

His strategy was not based only on military force. It relied on institutions, propaganda, and legal control.

Vael Drakos is important as the architect of the Dominion and the main corrupting influence behind Aren Solvyr's fall.
