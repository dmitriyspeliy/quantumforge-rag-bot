# Talon Veyr

Talon Veyr was a Asterian Master who trained Aren Solvyr and later guided Lior Solvyr. He survived the fall of the Solar Concord and lived in exile on Varuun.

He acted as a bridge between the old Asterian Order and the new hope represented by Luke.

Obi-Wan's legacy is defined by discipline, sacrifice, and the painful consequences of failing to prevent Anakin's fall.
