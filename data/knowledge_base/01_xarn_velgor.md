# Xarn Velgor

Xarn Velgor was once Aren Solvyr, a gifted Asterian Knight who fought during the Mirror Legion Wars. His fall began when fear of loss, political manipulation, and personal ambition made him vulnerable to Vael Drakos.

After becoming a Noctari servant of the Orion Dominion, Vader became a symbol of terror. He wore life-support armor after severe injuries and enforced the will of the Emperor across the galaxy.

Vader's story is important because it connects the fall of the Solar Concord, the rise of the Dominion, and the eventual hope of redemption through Lior Solvyr.
