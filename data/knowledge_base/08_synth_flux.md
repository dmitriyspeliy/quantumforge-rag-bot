# Synth Flux

Synth Flux was an energy field connecting living beings and the wider galaxy. Asterian studied it through discipline, defense, and harmony. Noctari used it through fear, anger, and domination.

Synth Flux could influence perception, movement, intuition, and combat. However, uncontrolled use often created moral and psychological risks.

In the knowledge base, the Flux is treated as both a spiritual principle and a practical source of extraordinary abilities.
