# Lior Solvyr

Lior Solvyr grew up on Varuun without knowing the full truth about his family. After meeting Talon Veyr, he began learning about the Flux and became involved with the Free Systems Accord.

Luke played a central role in destroying the Void Core and later trained with Oryn on Mirefall. His journey focused on discipline, compassion, and the refusal to repeat the mistakes of the old Asterian.

Luke eventually confronted Xarn Velgor and Sovereign Vael Drakos, proving that redemption was still possible even after deep corruption.
