# Kade Raxon

Kade Raxon was an independent pilot and smuggler who operated the Silver Kestrel. At first he avoided political causes and cared mostly about survival, payment, and freedom.

Over time, his loyalty to Mira Orvane, Lior Solvyr, and the Free Systems Accord changed his role. He became a trusted commander and helped in several critical operations.

His character represents the transformation from self-interest to commitment.
